## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne peppe

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, mix together all the spices with the olive oil to form a paste. 
2. Pat chicken dry and coat evenly with the seasoning mixture. 
3. Let sit for 10-15 minutes (or refrigerate up to 24 hours for more flavor). 
4. Grill, roast, or pan-sear chicken as desired until fully cooked.

### Notes
his blend works for chicken breasts, thighs, drumsticks, or wings. Also great on vegetables or potatoes! Adjust cayenne for more or less heat.
